Qt
--
The Windows installer, the macOS app and the Linux AppImage include the Qt 6 libraries
and plugins (https://www.qt.io), copyright The Qt Company Ltd. and other contributors,
used under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 (LGPL-3.0.txt, which adds
to the GPL in LICENSE.txt).

Meandris Write links to Qt dynamically, so you can replace the Qt libraries with your own
build. The source code of every Qt release is available at
https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/. Qt itself contains third-party components under their
own licences, listed at https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html.

The .deb and .rpm packages do not include Qt; they use the Qt from your distribution.
